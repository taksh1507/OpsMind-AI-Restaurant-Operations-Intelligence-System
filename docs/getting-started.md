# Getting Started with OpsMind

This guide walks a restaurant owner through setting up OpsMind from scratch.
It takes about 10 minutes. You do **not** need any technical background.

> **What you'll do:** create your restaurant account → add your menu →
> invite your staff → turn on AI insights. After that, OpsMind starts giving
> you daily tips and analytics based on *your* data.

---

## 1. Create your restaurant account

1. Open the OpsMind website and click **Sign up / Register**.
2. Enter:
   - **Restaurant name** (e.g. "The Copper Fork")
   - **Your email** — this becomes the **owner** login
   - **A password** (at least 8 characters)
3. Click **Create account**.

You are now the **owner**. The owner has full control: menu, staff, settings,
and billing. A private workspace is created just for your restaurant — no one
else can see your data.

---

## 2. Add your menu

Open **Menu** in the sidebar.

- Click **Add item** to add each dish: name, category, price, and (optionally)
  ingredient costs so OpsMind can calculate profit margins.
- Group items into **categories** (e.g. Burgers, Drinks, Desserts).
- You can mark items **unavailable** instead of deleting them (useful for
  seasonal dishes).

> **Tip:** the more accurate your prices and ingredient costs, the better the
> AI's margin and pricing advice will be.

---

## 3. Invite your staff

Open **Team** in the sidebar (owner only).

1. Click **Add team member** and enter their email and a **role**:
   - **Manager** — can see sales, insights, and analytics.
   - **Staff** — limited view of only what they need.
   - (**Owner** is you — there is one owner.)
2. OpsMind creates the account and shows a **one-time temporary password**.
3. Share that temporary password with your staff member securely (in person or
   over a trusted channel). It is shown **only once**.
4. On their first login, they are required to set their own new password.

> Roles decide what each person can see and do. You can change a person's role
> or remove them at any time from the Team page.

---

## 4. Set your preferences

Open **Settings** in the sidebar (owner only).

- **Restaurant name & timezone** — used across reports and daily tips.
- **Default city** — used for weather-aware recommendations.
- **AI model** — choose how powerful/fast the AI should be:
  - *Opus* — most capable, deepest analysis (default)
  - *Sonnet* — balanced speed and quality
  - *Haiku* — fastest and most economical
- **AI Insights** toggle — turn AI-generated tips and narratives on/off.
- **Weather Signals** toggle — factor local weather into daily tips.

Click **Save Changes**. Your choices apply immediately to all AI features.

---

## 5. Use your daily insights

Once your menu and some sales exist, OpsMind gives you:

- **Daily Tip** — "what should I push today?", weather- and trend-aware.
- **Revenue forecast** — expected revenue based on your trends.
- **Profit-margin analysis** — which items make/lose you money.
- **Review sentiment** — how customers feel, summarized.
- **Staffing efficiency** — labor vs. demand.

If a feature says it's **disabled**, check your Settings — AI Insights or
Weather may be turned off, or you may need to set a default city.

---

## Frequently asked

- **I forgot my password.** Ask your owner to remove and re-invite you, or use
  the account-recovery flow if enabled.
- **A staff member can't see Sales/Insights.** That's expected for the *Staff*
  role. Change them to *Manager* on the Team page if they need it.
- **The AI tip won't show.** Open Settings and confirm AI Insights and Weather
  are on and a default city is set.
